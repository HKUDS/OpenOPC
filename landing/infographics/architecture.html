Architecture · for contributors

One brief in, a finished delivery out — across seven layers

OpenOPC is a coordination runtime, not just an agent launcher. Like a company with departments, it splits the work into seven layers — who talks to you, who plans, who does the work, what tools they use, what they remember, and who watches it all. One file wires them together: opc/engine.py.

What happens when you send a brief

Briefyou type it (L0)→ Routetask or company? (L1)→ Planwork-item DAG (L2)→ Executeagents + tools (L3–L4)→ Gatereview · safety · human (L2)→ Deliver+ remember (L5–L6)

Two modes, one runtime. Task Mode = one agent does the job (fast). Company Mode = an org of role-agents runs the DAG with manager decompose → review → escalate. Same engine; the mode just decides how many players are on the field.
