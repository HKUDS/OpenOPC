Culture & incentives · the CNO's game

You can't order people to close loops — you design a game

Make gaps visible, experiments cheap and safe, verification independent, and payout automatic and proportional to verified compounding value. Top-down sets the rules; bottom-up plays. The CNO builds the kitchen — and never cooks.

1

The open kitchen — five mechanisms

Discover the open loop
→
An auto-surfaced gap board (a friction sensor + your readiness Nos + high-burden workflows). Nobody is told where gaps are.
Propose + experiment
→
A bounded closed-loop: hypothesis + one metric + rollback. Cheap to spin up; the safety gate keeps it off production.
Verify — the expo checks, not the cook
→
maker ≠ checker. An independent referee decides if the metric moved. You need the metric, not a manager's blessing.
Reward — the tip formula on the wall
→
An on-chain contract pays the verified close — two signatures, compounding vesting, safety-zero. Not the chef's mood.
See it compound
→
A public ledger of closes + their compounding curve. Seeing your fix lift the whole line is the intrinsic reward.
2

What the contract rewards (the crux)

✍️

maker ≠ checker

Two signatures on-chain. If the maker can attest their own result for money, they grade their own homework. Encoded in Solidity, not a policy doc.

📈

compounding vesting

The payout streams, and re-verifying durability extends it. A fix that keeps paying earns more than a one-shot — so people hunt leverage, not applause.

🔒

safety = hard zero

A safety failure zeroes the unreleased reward and halts — regardless of ROI. A terminal gate, never a weighted term.

Reference sketch (unaudited): contracts/VerifiedClosePayout.sol. Reward triggers on a verified close — never a proposal, never effort.

3

Grade your incentive system: L0 → L4

0–1 · L0 — Top-down

Gaps found by managers; improvement is assigned, not hunted.

2–3 · L1 — Suggestion box

People can propose, but nothing is verified or reliably rewarded.

4–5 · L2 — Refereed bounties

An independent judge + a safety zero; verified closes get paid (even by hand).

6–7 · L3 — Compounding culture

Payout rewards leverage; visibility is public; people hunt gaps unprompted.

8 · L4 — Self-incentivizing org

The game runs on-chain and un-gameable; the company closes its own loops while leadership designs the next.

Sequence it

Culture first, contract second. Pay the first verified close by hand; encode the math only after it's proven fair. Never code an incentive you haven't run by hand.
