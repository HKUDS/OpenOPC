Built for Physical AI

A robot that learns to work is one loop, turned fast

People teach a robot, the robot learns a brain, the brain is tested against reality, the robot goes to a real site, and what happens there becomes the next lesson. OpenOPC ships that loop as a runnable org — the physical-ai-robotics-company preset.

1

The robot-learning flywheel

👐 Teachteleop data→ 🧠 Learna VLA policy→ 🧪 Testsim2real + eval→ 🚀 Deployfield pilot→ 📡 Observefleet telemetry↺
Humans show the robot the task
→
Teleoperation demos formatted as (image, instruction, action) tuples (RLDS). Coverage beats raw count.
One model turns pixels into motion
→
A Vision-Language-Action (VLA) policy — behavior cloning, then flow-matching / diffusion for contact-rich motion.
Fail cheaply before spending hardware time
→
A sim of this robot+task (Isaac Sim / MuJoCo) + domain randomization; gate on held-out success rate on real hardware.
It isn't real until it works on the floor
→
A field pilot with safety envelopes and operator handoff (Robot-as-a-Service); telemetry becomes the next batch of teaching data.

The 10 role archetypes — one per arc of the loop

👑 Founding AI Native Lead final decider

Owns the capability thesis and the deployment go/no-go. Runs the company as an AI-native loop. The anchor role this repo is built around.

🧠 Robot Learning Lead

Imitation-vs-RL, the data budget, the training curriculum, and the success-rate bar a policy must clear.

🔬 Foundation Model Scientist

Trains the VLA / policy — behavior cloning, flow-matching, RL fine-tune. Owns the obs→action mapping.

📦 Data Engine Engineer

The teleop→dataset pipeline: collection protocol, RLDS formatting, dedup, coverage, the data-quality gate.

🧊 Sim2Real Engineer

The simulator of the specific robot+task, domain randomization, and the transfer eval.

👁️ Perception Engineer

3D vision, SLAM, state estimation — the world state the policy and planners consume.

🤚 Manipulation Engineer

Grasping and contact-rich skills — pick, place, press, twist, connect — with force awareness.

🛤️ Motion Planning Engineer

Trajectories, collision-free paths, real-time control, motion safety envelopes and cycle time.

🚀 Deployment & Field Engineer

Takes the robot to the site, runs the pilot, owns on-site reliability and the RaaS surface.

🛡️ AI Infra & Reliability Engineer

Eval harness, fleet telemetry, safety gates, uptime. Reviewer on the sim2real and deployment gates.

Prototype: RoboForce — generalized to the industry

The anchor is a real open job

RoboForce (Titan — an industrial robot for “dull, dirty, dangerous” work; ~$67M raised) is hiring a Founding AI Native Lead. OpenOPC ships that org as the roboforce-titan saved org.

Same loop, different robot bodies

The preset generalizes across the field — Physical Intelligence (π), Figure (Helix), 1X, Tesla Optimus, NVIDIA GR00T, Apptronik, Agility — each staffing the same flywheel.

“Founding AI Engineer”, the common denominator: end-to-end ownership of the whole loop — data → model → sim/eval → integration → deployment → telemetry → back to data — not a single slice. That is exactly what the founding_ai_native_lead final-decider role encodes.
