Company Mode · engineering contract

Two filing cabinets, one rule for who owns each fact

Company Mode keeps two records with different jobs. Think of them as two filing cabinets: one holds what the business decided, the other holds how the machine ran it. Put a fact in the wrong cabinet and the invariant tests fail.

work_item  DelegationWorkItem.metadata

The business cabinet: everything scheduling, the board, collaboration, review/report, and user-visible progress should read from.

runtime_task  Task.metadata

The execution-infrastructure cabinet: sessions, locks, recovery, runtime audit, replay. Never business facts.

2

Two rules that keep it honest

⚖️

Conflicts aren't silently repaired

WorkItem wins, and diagnostics report metadata_ownership_conflict. Invariants are enforced by work_item_runtime_invariants.py and its tests.

🔗

Linkage is not metadata

The WorkItem↔Task relation lives in the work_item_runtime_links table — never in metadata. UI payloads may expose it as runtime_task_id / execution_turn_id.

For the implementer: before adding a metadata key, check the matrix in metadata_ownership.py. A field on the wrong record fails invariant tests — the migrator migrate_work_item_owned_metadata_from_linked_tasks can dry-run or backfill WorkItem-owned values from legacy Tasks.
